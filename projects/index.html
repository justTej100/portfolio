Training Classifiers on Canadian Political Data

A machine learning report that trains a soft-margin support vector machine to predict which of Canada's 343 federal ridings elected a Liberal MP in the 2025 federal election, using census and demographic data.

It compares three optimization algorithms (simulated annealing, BFGS and AdaDelta) across three kernels (linear, RBF and polynomial) and two feature setups (raw standardized features and PCA). The best model, simulated annealing with a polynomial kernel on raw features, reached 72.5% test accuracy.
